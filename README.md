# Live Visitor Analytics – Real-Time Visitor Tracking, IP Address & Location

![Live Visitor Analytics](https://ps.w.org/pinnaclequant-com-analytics/assets/banner-1544x500.png?rev=3769745)

See who is on your WordPress site right now: IP address, city and state, ISP, screen size, browser, where they came from and the pages they read. Free, and it all stays in your own database. By PinnacleQuant.com.

**Install it from WordPress.org:** https://wordpress.org/plugins/pinnaclequant-com-analytics/

## What you see

One row per visitor, as they arrive:

* Whether they are on the site right now
* IP address
* City, state and country
* ISP or organization
* New or returning, with the visit number and first-seen date
* Where they came from: Google, Bing, social networks, AI assistants, ads (click IDs), UTM campaigns, or another site
* The page they are reading now and every page they read, with time on each
* Screen type and resolution, browser and operating system
* One-click Block from site for any address

Plus totals for today, yesterday, 7 days and 30 days, the bounce rate, a by-hour table for today, a 30-day table, a screen size breakdown, a Traffic flow page (entry pages, exit pages, next pages and the most common paths), a dashboard widget, a search box, a CSV export, and an email report every day or every week.

## Free live chat

Talk with the people on your site while they are on it. The free Live Chat add-on and the steps to set it up are at https://pinnaclequant.com/analytics-systems/

## Everything stays on your site

* No account, no key, no analytics service.
* Visitor details are written to tables in your own WordPress database and nowhere else.
* City, state and ISP are looked up on your own server from two free database files from DB-IP (https://db-ip.com), downloaded into your uploads folder with one click; after that, a fresh copy is fetched once a month. The files are provided under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
* Nothing in your visitors' browsers contacts any other server.
* Old visits are removed automatically after the number of days you choose (90 by default).

## Requirements

* WordPress 6.2 or newer
* PHP 7.4 or newer
* MySQL or MariaDB; also runs on SQLite (WordPress Playground)

## Included library

The plugin includes the MaxMind DB Reader for PHP (https://github.com/maxmind/MaxMind-DB-Reader-php), Apache License 2.0, to read the location files.
